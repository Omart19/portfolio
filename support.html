Before you report

Help me help you

  1. Check the fixes first. Each how-to guide on the S.P.I.D.E.R. page ends with fixes for common problems, like this one.
  2. Look for the same problem in the open reports. If it is there, add a comment instead of a new report.
  3. One problem per report. Two problems are two reports.
  4. Say how to make it happen again: the steps, the version, and what you expected.

Fair-use limits

So that a flood of reports cannot bury the real ones:

  • Reporting needs a free GitHub account.
  • Each account can send 3 reports in 24 hours. Extra reports are closed automatically with a note.
  • No more than 5 open reports per account at a time.
  • Reports from accounts less than a week old are marked for a manual check.
  • Only the forms can be used, so every report has the details needed.
  • If reporting is ever flooded, it is paused for a short time.
Reports are public. Anyone can read them. Do not post your email, home address, passwords, serial numbers, or logs that show your network or Bluetooth addresses. S.P.I.D.E.R.'s logs are in %APPDATA%\SPIDER\logs: blank out any addresses before you attach one.
