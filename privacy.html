Privacy

This site and S.P.I.D.E.R. are made by Oscar (OMART19) in California, USA. They collect as little as possible: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads.

What this website collects

The hire form. If you send it, I receive your name, email, company, website or job link, the kind of work, timeline, budget and message. The form is delivered by Formspree, a form service that emails it to me and keeps a copy so it can deliver it (see Formspree's privacy policy). I use it only to reply to you about work. I do not sell it, share it or use it for marketing, and I keep it only as long as the conversation needs. Ask me to delete it at any time.

Problem reports are filed on GitHub. They are public, and GitHub's privacy statement applies to them. Do not put personal details in a report.

Your browser stores one small item on your own device: the time you last sent the hire form, so it is not sent twice by accident. It never leaves your browser.

Nothing else is collected: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels.

Other services your browser talks to

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and the downloads. Like any web host it sees your IP address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on these pages; Google sees your IP address when they load.
  • Links to YouTube, Patreon, eBay, Instagram, TikTok and Steam take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

S.P.I.D.E.R. (the program)

S.P.I.D.E.R. collects no personal data. When it checks for updates, it downloads one public file from this site; nothing about you or your computer is sent, and you can turn the check off in its settings. Robots and boards connect directly to your own computer.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask what I have about you (at most: a hire form message) and ask me to correct or delete it.
  • Do Not Track: this site does not track you, so a Do Not Track signal changes nothing here.
  • This site is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not send the hire form.

To ask about your data, use the hire form or reach me through the links on the contact section.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
