Don't Stop Walking

A purple storm is coming down the road, and it does not stop. Gather your crew, head out through the portal, hunt and craft on the move, and get back to base with whatever you can carry.

The loop

Walk, take, get out

Co-op

Six players, one outpost

Every player has a base in the shared hub. When a friend joins, their base glides in; when they leave, it goes. Nothing reloads.

Runs

A new road every time

Step through the portal and the run is generated fresh: canyon walls, forest, wildlife, and the storm behind you.

Survive

Hunt, chop, craft

Stone axes, knives, bows and slingshots. Hunt deer, cut wood, and craft gear from what you find on the way.

Inventory

Pack it right

A grid backpack where every item has a shape and a weight. Pouches add room.

Extract

Bring it home

Make it out of a run and what you carried lands in the extraction box at your base.

Physics

Ragdolls and the Grabber

Full ragdoll physics, and a monster with a giant claw that drags your teammates away while you fight it off.

Behind the game

Made by one person

Design, code, multiplayer, models and animation are all mine. The game runs on Godot 4 with C#, with networking built for a six-player party where everyone can come and go.

To test that, I built bots that play the multiplayer in fifteen party shapes and flag every desync and duplicated item, and a capture rig that plays the game to shoot these screenshots.
