Build 01 · running

Servo test rig

The first robot: one servo on an ESP32-S3, powered from a breadboard module, connected to S.P.I.D.E.R. over Bluetooth with no pairing.

Spec sheet

Board
ESP32-S3 (YD-ESP32-S3 N8R2)
Servo
MG90S or SG90, signal on GPIO 4
Power
Breadboard module at 5 V, ground shared with the board
Link
Bluetooth LE to S.P.I.D.E.R., back online about 5 s after a reset
Firmware
MicroPython with my robot link and servo script

The servo follows the dashboard slider live, sweeps, centres and lets go. A Check signal button makes the board measure the pulse on its own pin and sense whether a wire is attached, so wiring faults show up on screen.
